Halved It

Halved It (formerly named Inside Everything) is a YouTube channel of short, original 3D animations that show how everyday things work on the inside: locks, light bulbs, batteries, aeroplane windows, spacecraft and more.

Inside Everything uploader

"Inside Everything uploader" is a small private tool used only by the channel's owner. It uploads the channel's own finished videos to the channel's own YouTube account through the YouTube Data API, with their titles, descriptions and scheduled publish times. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. See our privacy policy for how data is handled.
